# Portfolio evidence checklist

For each project, a reviewer should be able to answer:

- What user problem does it solve, and what is outside scope?
- What did you implement personally? What came from existing libraries or collaborators?
- Where did the data come from, and may it be used/shared?
- Can a fresh environment reproduce the documented small example?
- What baseline did you compare, and why is the chosen metric appropriate?
- What happens on bad input, missing data or an unavailable dependency/API?
- What results were actually observed, and what remains untested?
- How would you improve it with more time, data or compute?

A useful five-minute presentation: one minute on problem/data, one on design, one on demonstration, one on evaluation/failures, one on tradeoffs and next steps. Include clear screenshots or logs with sensitive values removed. Avoid inflated accuracy claims, fictitious clients and unsupported production-readiness labels.

During interview practice, change a project assumption: smaller dataset, new class, slower API, missing label or stricter latency. Explain what you would change and how you would validate it.
