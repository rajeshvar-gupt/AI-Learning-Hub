# STAT-001 · Worked solutions

1. Mean = 22, median = 3. The median better represents the center of these observations; the mean remains useful for totals or expected costs.
2. SE = 2; at n=144 it is 1, assuming independent comparable observations.
3. Paying customers are a selected population. Free users and people who stopped using the product may differ systematically; increasing this selected sample does not remove that bias.
