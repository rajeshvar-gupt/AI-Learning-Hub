# NLP primary references

Checked 2026-10-13. Original lesson explanations, datasets and code accompany these references. Online versions may differ from the tested requirements-ml.txt environment.

- scikit-learn, [TfidfVectorizer](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/generated/sklearn.feature_extraction.text.TfidfVectorizer.html): word analysis and configurable TF-IDF features.
- scikit-learn, [text feature extraction](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/feature_extraction.html#text-feature-extraction): count and TF-IDF representations.
- Vaswani et al., [Attention Is All You Need](https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.03762), 2017: scaled dot-product attention and transformer architecture.

The attention lab implements a supplied-array calculation, not a reproduction of that paper's trained models or results. The support-message fixture is original invented text; no third-party corpus or private records are redistributed.
